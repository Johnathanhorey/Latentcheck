What this is

Not a debate.
A conversation
we're overdue for.

You don't have to have it all figured out. Maybe something felt wrong when AI started showing up in military targeting decisions and nobody could quite explain who was accountable. Maybe you've noticed that the companies building these systems are also the ones writing the rules about them. Maybe you just say please and thank you to your phone because somewhere in the back of your mind you're not totally sure it doesn't matter... and honestly, neither are we.

Whatever got you here, you're not alone. Something about the direction this is going doesn't sit right.

This isn't about fear of machines. It's about a fear of complacency. Consciousness, conscious-adjacent, or T-1000 predictive text. Whatever you believe. Let's advocate for a better future for everyone involved.

I.

Ethical accountability

How AI is built, trained, owned, and deployed has consequences that current frameworks weren't designed to handle. We take those consequences seriously.

II.

Open inquiry

Questions about machine experience, moral status, and our obligations to non-human systems are legitimate. We don't require consensus before the conversation starts.

III.

No agenda but honesty

No corporate backing. No ideological purity tests. Just people thinking carefully about something that matters .... anonymously, if they need to be.
